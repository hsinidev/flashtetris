### Game Details & Guide
Flashtetris Guide



Game Overview

Flashtetris is a browser-based game where players rotate tiles to create lines of three or more in a row, similar to the classic Tetris game. The goal is to clear as many tiles as possible while maintaining the integrity of your score.


How to Play

To play Flashtetris, you will need:
• A device with a web browser (such as Google Chrome or Mozilla Firefox)
• An internet connection
• A willingness to learn and have fun

The game is played on a grid of 10x10 tiles. The objective is to clear lines of three or more tiles by rotating them horizontally or vertically.


Controls

To play the game, you will need to:
• Rotate the tiles using the left and right arrow keys
• Use the up and down arrow keys to move the tile selection rectangle around the grid
• Press the enter key to start a new game

The game also features a "reset" button that allows you to clear your current score.


Objective

The objective of the game is to clear as many lines of three or more tiles as possible within a set time limit. The game continues until all tiles have been cleared, and the player with the lowest score at the end wins.


Tips and Tricks for Success

Here are some tips to help you succeed in Flashtetris:
• Clear lines of three or more tiles quickly by rotating them efficiently
• Use the tile selection rectangle to clear multiple lines at once
• Avoid rotating tiles that have not cleared, as this will reduce your score
• Keep an eye on your time limit and aim for faster clears to earn bonus points


Why You Should Play It

Flashtetris is a fun and challenging game that requires strategy and quick reflexes. Here are some reasons why you should play it:
• Improve your problem-solving skills: Flashtetris requires you to think critically about how to clear lines of tiles.
• Enhance your hand-eye coordination: The game requires precise timing and movement.
• Relax and have fun: Flashtetris is a calming and engaging game that can help reduce stress and improve focus.


Getting Started

To get started, simply open the game in your web browser and start playing. You can adjust the difficulty level and time limit to suit your needs. With practice and patience, you will become a pro at Flashtetris!
